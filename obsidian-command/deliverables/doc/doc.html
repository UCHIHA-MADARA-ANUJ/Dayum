Section 01 · The Idea

The Idea

◈ 100-Word Summary

The Jedi are scattered, but not extinct. OBSIDIAN is a classified Imperial command portal that fuses a disorganized purge into a single hunting organism: a live galaxy tracker with probe drops, wanted dossiers with psychoprofiles, a kanban operations board, intelligence that escalates into hunts, a propaganda studio that disrupts Jedi recruitment, and encrypted Inquisitor comms. Built as a fully original, offline-first web app with its own design system, custom 3D renders from a self-written raytracer, and a 62-second trailer with an original score — the hunt, finally coordinated.

Why This Wins

FunctionalityEvery module is live and demoable — radar sweeps, drag-drop dispatch, a working terminal, chat that replies. Judges don't read about the app; they operate it.
UI/UXOne cinematic design system — "Iron Protocol" — with a gate, boot sequence, scramble typography, and a full in-app standards page.
AestheticObsidian black, imperial red, serif display type, scanline-free clean surfaces. Feels institutional and inevitable.
OriginalityZero canon characters, zero templates: nine original Jedi, ten sectors, six Inquisitors, seven lore documents, an original raytracing engine, an original score.

Section 02 · The Problem

The Problem

"Order 66 has fallen. Hundreds of survivors hide across a thousand worlds. They are rebuilding. They are recruiting. They are learning to vanish. The Empire has legions, but no eyes." — THE BRIEF · PATH 2

After the purge, the Empire's coordination collapsed:

FailureConsequence
Fragmented intelEach sector files reports into a void — no single picture of the galaxy.
Isolated InquisitorsSix hunters, six separate hunts. No shared channel, no joint doctrine.
Unprotected recruitment lanesThe Jedi recruit through refugee networks, and nothing intercepts them.
No decision layerIntel never becomes action: sightings sit in ledgers while targets slip away.

The Opportunity

The brief grants total creative freedom. We built the nervous system the Empire never had — a single classified network where every sector, every probe, and every citizen becomes a sensor in the Emperor's hand.

Section 04 · Technology

Architecture & Technology

Primary build — Next.js 15

Next.js 15React 19Framer MotionGSAPLenisThree.jsZustandTailwindSonnerLucide
3D — Custom RaytracerAll 3D renders come from a vectorized numpy raytracer written from scratch: spheres, boxes, cylinders, glow shells, reflections, starfields, tonemapping and vignette grading. Eight scenes, zero external 3D software.
Audio — SynthesizedThe film score ("Iron Protocol") and every UI sound are generated with code — WebAudio oscillators and WAV synthesis. No licensed tracks anywhere.
Film — Built by PipelineThe 62-second trailer assembles 3D renders, real app footage (recorded via headless browser), title cards and a voiceover through an ffmpeg conductor script. Fully reproducible.
Security ModelPassphrase gate with incident logging on failure, offline-first operation, self-hosted fonts, zero external calls. The interface itself is the threat theater.

Fallback build

A zero-dependency vanilla HTML/CSS/JS version ships alongside the Next.js app — the product runs from a single folder on any static host, anywhere, forever.

Deployment

One click to Vercel: the web/ folder is a standard Next.js project; import from GitHub and it deploys in minutes with a shareable judge link.

Nothing in OBSIDIAN references canon. Every name, doctrine and glyph is original to our universe.
