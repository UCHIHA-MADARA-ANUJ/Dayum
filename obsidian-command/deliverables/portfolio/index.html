The Project

01

Brief

Post-Order 66, the Empire lacks coordination. OBSIDIAN answers the brief's Empire path: global tracking, planning, and communication — plus our own layer: disrupting Jedi recruitment through propaganda and loyalty enforcement.

Our Spin

Every feature is demoable live: radar sweeps, probe drops, drag-and-drop operation dispatch, a broadcast studio that composes sector-wide propaganda, and six original Inquisitor characters who reply in-character.

Originality

No canon characters, no templates, no borrowed assets. Nine original Jedi, ten original sectors, original sigil system, original lore documents, original raytracing engine, original score.

Tech

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS web app — offline-first, self-hosted fonts, zero external calls. Custom vectorized Python raytracer for 3D. Synthesized audio. Headless-browser-driven film production.

03

Design System

The app ships its own 'Imperial Standards' page: 14 color tokens, 3 font families (Cinzel display, Rajdhani UI, Plex Mono data), component specs, motion tokens, and voice guidelines — judges can open it live inside the product.

Experience

Boot sequence with passphrase gate sets the fiction in the first 3 seconds. Scanlines, CRT vignette, radar sweeps, pulsing blips, typewriter broadcasts — every motion reinforces surveillance and inevitability.
