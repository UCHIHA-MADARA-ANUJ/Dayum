# OBSIDIAN — 100-Word Write-Up (3 options)

## Option A — "The Nervous System"
The Jedi are scattered, but not extinct. OBSIDIAN is a classified Imperial command portal that fuses a disorganized purge into a single hunting organism: a live galaxy tracker with probe drops, wanted dossiers with psychoprofiles, a kanban operations board, intelligence that escalates into hunts, a propaganda studio that disrupts Jedi recruitment, and encrypted Inquisitor comms. Built as a fully original, offline-first web app with its own design system, custom 3D renders from a self-written raytracer, and a 62-second trailer with an original score — the hunt, finally coordinated.

## Option B — "One Empire, One Will"
After Order 66, survivors rebuild in secret. OBSIDIAN gives the Empire the one weapon it lacks — coordination. Our portal unites sector tracking, target dossiers, operation dispatch, counter-recruitment propaganda, and Inquisitor communication into one classified network. Every feature is demoable, every asset original: a custom design system, 3D renders from our own raytracing engine, and a cinematic trailer with original music. The Empire no longer hunts blind. The hunt never ends.

## Option C — "Twelve Blades"
OBSIDIAN is the Empire's answer to a scattered enemy: a classified web portal where every sector, probe, and citizen becomes part of one hunt. Live galaxy tracking, psychoprofiled dossiers, a kanban operations board, intelligence-to-ops escalation, recruitment-disrupting broadcasts, and encrypted Inquisitor comms. Everything — design system, 3D renders, score, film — is original work. Twelve blades. One purpose. The hunt never ends.
