The Idea · ≤100 Words

The Idea

◈ OFFICIAL SUBMISSION — 100 WORDS

The Jedi are scattered, but not extinct. OBSIDIAN is a classified Imperial command portal that fuses a disorganized purge into a single hunting organism: a live galaxy tracker with probe drops, wanted dossiers with psychoprofiles, a kanban operations board, intelligence that escalates into hunts, a propaganda studio that disrupts Jedi recruitment, and encrypted Inquisitor comms. Built as a fully original, offline-first web app with its own design system, custom 3D renders from a self-written raytracer, and a 62-second trailer with an original score — the hunt, finally coordinated.

Context · The Problem

The Problem

"Order 66 has fallen. Hundreds of survivors hide across a thousand worlds. They are rebuilding. They are recruiting. They are learning to vanish. The Empire has legions, but no eyes." — The Brief · Path 2

After the purge, the Empire's coordination collapsed in four ways:

FailureConsequence
Fragmented intelEach sector files reports into a void — no single picture of the galaxy.
Isolated InquisitorsSix hunters, six separate hunts. No shared channel, no joint doctrine.
Unprotected recruitment lanesThe Jedi recruit through refugee networks, and nothing intercepts them.
No decision layerIntel never becomes action: sightings sit in ledgers while targets slip away.

The Opportunity

The brief grants total creative freedom. We built the nervous system the Empire never had — a single classified network where every sector, every probe, and every citizen becomes a sensor in the Emperor's hand.
